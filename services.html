What we actually do.

Three services that work better together than apart: the build, the search presence, and the writing that keeps both alive.

01 — Build

Web Development

From ₹999

We build fast, functional websites from scratch. Clean front-end architecture, responsive layouts across every device, and strong performance out of the box — no page builders, no plugin stacks, nothing you cannot maintain later.

The process is straightforward: scope, build in short passes with a live link at each stage, then launch with performance and metadata checks. You get the source and the hosting account.

  • Single-page and multi-page builds
  • Custom UI and interaction design
  • Responsive across every breakpoint
  • Performance and accessibility passes
  • Metadata, sitemap and indexing setup
  • Deployment and full handover

02 — Discover

Search Engine Optimization

From ₹499

Most sites do not have a keyword problem — they have a plumbing problem. Pages that are not indexed, titles that say nothing, no sitemap, a robots file blocking the one thing that matters. We fix that before anyone starts chasing rankings.

Once the groundwork is sound, the on-page work follows: metadata, headings, internal links, structured data and a keyword map built around real search intent. Everything is reported in plain language.

  • Technical audits and indexing checks
  • Title tags, meta and headings
  • Keyword research by intent
  • Schema and structured data
  • Local and Google Business setup
  • Monthly monitoring and reports
