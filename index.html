02 — Discover

Search Engine Optimization

From ₹499

Most sites do not have a keyword problem — they have a plumbing problem. Pages that are not indexed, titles that say nothing, no sitemap, a robots file blocking the one thing that matters. We fix that before anyone starts chasing rankings.

Once the groundwork is sound, the on-page work follows: metadata, headings, internal links, structured data and a keyword map built around real search intent. Everything is reported in plain language.

Questions

About the numbers.

Do I own the code and the hosting?
Yes. Hosting, domains and any third-party accounts are set up in your name, and the source is handed over at the end of the project. There is no lock-in.
How long does a website take?
A single-page build is usually ready within a week. Multi-page sites take one to two weeks, and custom UI work depends on scope — we give you a date before starting.
Where should I start with SEO?
With the ₹499 audit. It tells you what is actually broken — indexing, metadata, speed, structure — before you spend on anything else. Most sites only need the audit plus one or two follow-up scopes.
Can I combine services?
Yes, and it usually works better that way. A new build plus on-page SEO and a content calendar is the most common combination, since the three reinforce each other.
How do payments work?
One-off projects are split into two payments — half to start, half on delivery. Monthly services are billed at the beginning of each cycle and can be stopped with 30 days notice.

Need something not listed?

Most projects fit one of these scopes. If yours does not, describe it and we will quote it as a fixed price rather than an hourly rate.

Kimi Technologies started as a publication about AI and robotics. It became a studio because writing about a technology only gets you so far.

The story

Writers who build.
Builders who write.

Kimi Technologies started in 2023 as an independent publication covering artificial intelligence and robotics. The premise was simple: most coverage of the field is either too shallow to be useful or too technical to be read.

Writing about a technology only gets you so far, so we started building with it — first tools, then websites for other people. That is still the shape of the studio. We are developers who write, and writers who build.

When we recommend a technical SEO fix or a content plan, it comes from work we have already done on our own products.

Every project we release publicly is free, client-side and open source. It keeps us honest about performance, privacy and the cost of unnecessary complexity — the same standards we hold client work to.

We work with small teams, independent founders and the occasional larger group that wants one accountable partner for the build, the search presence and the writing.

How we work

Three rules we keep.

01

No black boxes

Fixed scope, fixed price, and a written note on what is included before work starts. If something changes, you hear about it before the invoice does.

02

Boring technology wins

Static files, semantic HTML and as little JavaScript as the job allows. It loads faster, breaks less, and anyone can pick it up later.

03

Privacy is a feature

No trackers, no third-party scripts we would not defend, and no client data stored anywhere it does not need to be.

Home/Privacy

Privacy Policy

Last updated 29 October 2026

Kimi Technologies keeps this website simple, and that extends to data. There is no login, no account and no tracking script. This page explains the little that applies.

What we collect

Nothing, unless you choose to write to us. This site has no forms, no cookies for tracking and no analytics. If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you include in the message.

How we use it

Email is used to reply to your enquiry and, if a project goes ahead, to run it. We do not add you to a mailing list, and we do not sell or share contact details with third parties.

Third parties

This site is served as static files and loads typefaces from Google Fonts, which may log the request. Our open-source tools — Fácil, BeeLink, OPENQR, NOT ACCESS and CLIPO — are hosted separately on Vercel and Netlify and each run entirely in your browser; no file you process in them is uploaded to us.

Cookies and local storage

This site sets no cookies. Two preferences are stored in your browser's local and session storage on your device: your colour theme, and the animation used for the transition between pages. Neither is transmitted anywhere.

Your rights

Because we hold no personal data beyond correspondence you initiate, there is nothing to request or delete. If you would like an email exchange removed, write to connect.kimitech@gmail.com and we will do so.
